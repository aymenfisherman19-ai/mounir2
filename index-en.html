Nutritionist Tips

💧 Hydration
Drink at least 3 to 3.5 litres of water per day. As a mechanic with high physical activity, dehydration will hurt your performance and slow fat burning. Start every morning with a large glass of water before your first meal.
🍳 Meal Prep (Sunday)
Cook chicken, rice, and vegetables in large batches every Sunday. Divide them in advance into sealed containers. This makes hitting your macros non-negotiable during busy workdays.
⚙️ Carb Timing
Focus most of your carbs around your workout (lunch + pre-workout). On rest days, cut pre-workout carbs by 50% — swap the banana for vegetables or a smaller portion.
🥩 Beef vs Chicken Rotation
Beef is capped at 200g/day. On days without beef, replace it with: 200g extra chicken breast, or 150g canned sardines to boost Omega-3. Include sardines 2–3 times per week.
🚫 Foods to Avoid
Merguez, fried foods, Algerian pastries (baklava, zlabia), sodas, too much harissa, excess white bread. These foods will silently break your deficit without feeling like you've cheated.
🌿 Free Vegetables
The following vegetables can be eaten freely (no counting needed): lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, zucchini, green pepper, spinach, fresh herbs (coriander, parsley), lemon juice. They add volume, fiber, and minerals for almost zero calories.
